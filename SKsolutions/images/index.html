Our Founder

Meet the visionary behind our journey toward IP excellence and innovation.

Satatham Kritam Legal Solution LLP (SKLS)

“Continuum of actions or creations of excellence”

Dr. S K Murthy

Satatham Kritam, the two words together, mean “Continuum of actions or creations of excellence.” The name of the Firm is an inspirational and logical basis for building the culture of Satatham Kritam Legal Solution LLP (SKLS). SKLS was founded by Dr. S K Murthy (an engineer and a lawyer with about 30 years of experience in technology, IPR, IP policy, corporate governance, and teaching in MNCs – last 20+ years in Intel India as its Director of IP) in Dec 2024 as an LLP.

SKLS brings a unique offering to the practice of IP by collaborating with innovators and innovative companies (including multi-national companies having R&D facilities in India) to build a strategy for protecting their valuable IP. SKLS collaborates with clients to create an IP protection strategy by planning and conducting IP harvesting sessions, helping in-house counsels identify high-value inventions, and assisting inventors in creating high-quality invention disclosures.

Further, SKLS offers to collaborate with inventors and innovative companies to devise effective IP protection strategies. Offerings include preparing high-quality patent applications, prosecuting patent applications, preparing claim charts, protecting trademarks, designs, and copyrights in cutting-edge technology areas.
